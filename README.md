## About

I'm a Mechatronics Engineer working at the intersection of embedded firmware, avionics hardware, and control theory. I design and validate control systems in research and industry — from a robotics lab at **AIST** in Tokyo to firmware for **VantTec's** autonomous vehicles — and now run an independent practice delivering full-cycle hardware-software products, from PCB to firmware to validated control, for clients in industrial, medical, and agricultural sectors.

**🌐 Portfolio site — coming soon.**

---

## What I Do

**Embedded Firmware** — real-time control for STM32/ESP32, from motor control to safety-critical logic, validated through HIL/SIL testing.

**PCB & Avionics Design** — multilayer PCBs in Altium, including UAV/drone avionics: flight controllers, ESCs, companion computers.

**Control Systems Validation** — PID/LQR/MPC design, proven on Quanser HIL rigs and in MATLAB/Simulink before it ever touches real hardware.

## Projects

It's one arc, not a pile of one-offs: control theory, proven on hardware, then flown.

It started on the ground — a scaled **[Ackermann autonomous vehicle](https://github.com/carlos2219/PID_Ackermann_Car)** on STM32, fusing IMU and ultrasonic data for closed-loop navigation. That control foundation moved into the air with **[MPC trajectory control for a Quanser quadrotor](https://github.com/carlos2219/MPC_Drone_quanser)**, validated on Hardware-in-the-Loop before any real flight. From there into avionics, leading the electronics subsystem for Tec de Monterrey's entry in **SAE AeroDesign Mexico**, and into a payload computer for UAVs with **[ESP32-based flight payload firmware](https://github.com/carlos2219/ESP32-UAV-payload)**.

Currently designing a UAV flight-controller PCB in Altium — schematic to flight-ready hardware.
